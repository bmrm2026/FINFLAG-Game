Key Finfluencer Evaluation Rule:
«Before accepting or sharing financial content from a finfluencer, always check the claim, evidence, source, credibility, possible bias, risks, and missing information.»

🛡️ Finfluencer Content Evaluation Checklist

  • Claim Check: Does the influencer make bold guarantees or promise wealth without disclosing risks?
  • Evidence Check: Are they showing verified statistics, or just past personal profit screenshots and follower numbers?
  • Source Check: Is the entity licensed by the SEC Philippines or supervised by the BSP? DTI registration alone does not permit taking investments.
  • Bias & Sponsorship: Is it a paid partnership, affiliate promo code, or pump-and-dump promotion?
  • Think Before Sharing: If an offer relies on recruiting new members (like online paluwagan) or urges urgency within minutes, never share it with peers.
